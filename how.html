The game in 30 secondsA fast, chaotic obstacle-course race for up to 16 players.

Pick your character, join a race and run to the finish line as fast as you can. Along the way you dodge spinning hammers, bounce off bumpers, ride conveyor belts and slides, cross ice and falling tiles — and grab power-up boxes to slow down, swap with or kick the racers ahead of you.

🏁 Goal

Cross the finish line before the others. In free races the first 8 to finish qualify; the rest are out of that round.

👥 Up to 16 players

Play with friends online. In free races, bots fill the empty spots so the race is always full.

🔁 Round after round

After the results table, the next course starts automatically. Courses are long and each one mixes different obstacles and slides.

ObstaclesEvery course is a different mix.

🔨 Hammers

Spinning bars knock you back. Time your jump over low bars or wait for a gap.

🟠 Bumpers

Bouncy pads that launch you sideways. Great for chaos, terrible for your line.

🎢 Slides

Climb a trampoline, then slide down at high speed — with bumpers and hammers waiting on the way.

➡ Conveyors

Moving belts that carry you (usually forward — sometimes with a surprise).

🧊 Ice & moving blocks

Slippery floors and platforms that slide across your path.

🟦 Falling tiles

Tiles drop shortly after you step on them. Don't stop.
Fall off? No problem. You respawn at the last checkpoint you passed (the flags on the rest platforms).

Play for prizeReal players only. The winner takes a card pack. Everyone shares the cost.

In one sentence

You and the other players split the price of one card pack; whoever finishes first wins it, and the card is sent straight to their wallet.

Step by step

1
Choose “Play for prize” and connect your walletYou need a Solana wallet such as Phantom. Connecting only shares your public address — the game never sees your secret keys.
2
Join a lobby — it's freeNothing is charged when you join. The lobby waits for at least 2 real players (up to 16). There are no bots in prize races.
3
The lobby locks and the price is splitWhen there are enough players, you get 20 seconds for more to join (more players = cheaper for everyone). Then the lobby locks and the pack price is divided between all players.
4
Everyone pays their share in USDCYou have 60 seconds to approve one payment in your wallet. You pay in USDC (a dollar-pegged coin) and you don't need any SOL — the game covers the network fee.
5
Race!When everyone has paid, the race starts. Same game, same controls — only real players, so every position counts.
6
The winner gets the cardThe first player to cross the finish line wins. The game buys the pack and the card is delivered directly to the winner's wallet — usually within seconds.

What it costs

The prize is a Starter Pack from Collector Crypt (price $25). The total is split equally, rounded up to the next cent:

PlayersYou payTotal collected

The price is fixed at the moment the lobby locks. Joining the lobby is always free.

What you can win

Packs hold one random card, drawn with a provably fair random roll (verifiable on Collector Crypt). Most cards are common; some are rare and worth much more.

Important: the card is random, so it can be worth more or less than what the pack costs. Winning is never a guaranteed profit.
  • The card is a digital collectible (NFT) backed by a real physical card stored in Collector Crypt's vault.
  • You can keep it, redeem the physical card (shipping) or sell it back to Collector Crypt within 72 hours for part of its insured value — all from collectorcrypt.com with your wallet.
